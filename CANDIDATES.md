# Time machine warning candidates (draft pass)

One warning label each, at most 120 characters.

1. Meet yourself: "WARNING: Do not meet yourself. One of you
   will want a photo; the other, plausible deniability." --
   character comedy, good.
2. Warranty and alibi: "WARNING: Returning five minutes
   early voids the warranty, the alibi, and in two
   jurisdictions, the marriage." -- escalating legal absurdity;
   the strongest label voice of the set.
3. Grandfather: "CAUTION: Grandfather paradox coverage sold
   separately. Grandfathers not included." -- insurance humour.
4. Lottery: "NOTICE: Lottery numbers from the future are
   considered insider trading in 2149." -- neat.
5. Spoilers: "WARNING: Do not tell past-you anything.
   Past-you cannot be trusted with spoilers. Source: you." --
   the "Source: you" tag is excellent.

## Verdict

Winner: candidate 2. A warning label should sound like it
was written by a legal department that has seen things, and
candidate 2 escalates exactly like one: warranty, alibi,
marriage -- each clause more personal and less insurable
than the last. Five minutes early has never carried so much
liability.
